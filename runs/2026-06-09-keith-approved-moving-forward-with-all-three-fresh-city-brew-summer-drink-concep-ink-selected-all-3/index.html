IDEA 01 · Instagram Reel / Facebook Reel · Cold sensory product Reel

The Condensation Test

A thumb-stopping macro Reel that proves the drink is cold and craveable before the viewer even reads the drink name. The concept sells summer refreshment through condensation, ice, tactile sound, and a City Brew handoff instead of generic 'cool off' copy.

Visual Direction / Mockup

Create a 6-8 second 9:16 Reel: macro condensation beads on an iced City Brew cup; ice clink/cup set-down/straw or lid snap; drink swirl or texture; the cup leaves a cold ring on the counter; final City Brew logo cup or handoff end card. Natural counter/window light is enough if the cup visibly looks cold. Use crisp tactile audio if available.

On-Asset Copy

If the cup doesn’t sweat, is it even summer? / Cold proof: passed. / [APPROVED DRINK NAME] at City Brew.

Caption Sample

If your summer drink passes the condensation test, you’re in the right place. Try [APPROVED DRINK NAME] on your next City Brew stop.

Assets Needed

  • One approved iced summer drink
  • Macro footage/photo of condensation, ice, lid/straw, drink texture
  • City Brew cup/logo visible for final frame
  • Optional handoff shot
  • Approval of exact drink name before final export

IDEA 02 · Instagram Stories sequence + Instagram/Facebook carousel · Interactive order-helper

Choose Your Chill Level

A useful Story/carousel order-helper that turns City Brew’s summer drink lineup into clear craving lanes: light refresh, coffee cold, or treat mode. It helps customers pick quickly without feeling like a menu board.

Visual Direction / Mockup

Build a four-frame IG Story: opening lineup with 'Choose your chill level'; poll for Light refresh vs Treat mode; poll/quiz for Coffee-forward vs Sweet + creamy; lane reveal mapping each lane to an approved drink; final screenshot/save CTA. Carousel backup: cover, one slide per lane, final 'Save this for your next City Brew stop.' Keep large type and clean product cards, not crowded menu-board design.

On-Asset Copy

Choose your chill level. / Light refresh. / Coffee cold. / Treat mode. / Screenshot your summer lane.

Caption Sample

Not sure where to start with summer drinks? Pick your chill level, save your lane, and make the next City Brew stop easy.

Assets Needed

  • 2-3 approved summer drink photos or one lineup shot
  • Approved taste notes/flavor lanes for each drink
  • Brand assets for simple lane-card design
  • Poll/quiz sticker guidance for Story frames
  • Approval that lanes do not imply unapproved customizations

IDEA 03 · Instagram Reel / Facebook Reel · Routine/lifestyle reset Reel

The 2 PM Melt

A real-life summer routine Reel where the day starts melting — work, errands, heat, scattered tasks — and a City Brew iced drink becomes the small afternoon reset. This makes the summer drink feel useful and emotionally true, not just pretty.

Visual Direction / Mockup

Create a 7-10 second 9:16 Reel: sticky notes/laptop/receipt/errand bag to open with 'When summer hits at 2 PM'; sunglasses/keys/fan/packed-day props showing the day feeling full; City Brew drink lands on counter/desk/table; macro condensation/ice/sip/handoff; final product hero with City Brew logo and drink name. Keep it adult/real and not chaotic-parent content.

On-Asset Copy

When summer hits at 2 PM… / The melt is real. / Reset secured. / [APPROVED DRINK NAME] — your afternoon reset.

Caption Sample

Some summer days need a second start. Make [APPROVED DRINK NAME] your afternoon reset on the next City Brew stop.

Assets Needed

  • One approved iced summer drink
  • Desk/counter/table setting
  • Props: sticky notes, receipt, keys, sunglasses, fan, tote, summer bag, laptop
  • Optional handoff or sip moment
  • Approval of drink name and availability/date limits

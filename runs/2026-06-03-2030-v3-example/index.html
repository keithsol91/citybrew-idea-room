Idea 01 · Reel/Story · Perks

Your Usual Should Count

Show a regular coffee run and make Perks feel like a natural add-on, not a rewards-program ad.

Visual direction / mockup

Close-up cup handoff, receipt/register moment, warm counter texture.

On-asset copy

“Your usual should count.” / “Already making the stop? Start earning from it.”

Caption sample

If City Brew is already part of your routine, make that routine count with Perks.

Assets needed
  • Cup handoff b-roll
  • Register/receipt shot
  • Approved Perks language
  • Fallback: cup photo + text overlay
Room notes: Moxie killed “Join Perks today” as too generic. Riff reframed it around an existing habit.

Idea 02 · Reel/Story · Product help

Cold But Not Too Sweet?

A useful barista recommendation that solves a real ordering moment instead of just announcing a drink.

Visual direction / mockup

Barista POV, ice/pour macro, drink hero. Helpful, not over-produced.

On-asset copy

“Cold but not too sweet?” / “Order this.” / approved drink name.

Caption sample

Need a cold order that still tastes like coffee? Ask for [approved drink name].

Assets needed
  • Approved featured drink
  • Pour/build b-roll
  • One sensory descriptor
  • Fallback: static product photo
Room notes: Moxie liked the usefulness. Riff says this cannot move until the exact drink is confirmed.
AI visual mockup — direction only, not final asset.
Idea 03 · Carousel/photo set · Local ritual

Meet Me At The Usual Table

Make City Brew feel like a real meeting place by anchoring content in a recognizable table/store moment.

Visual direction / mockup

Real table surface, two drinks, hands, laptop/book/keys. Warm and lived-in.

On-asset copy

Slide 1: “Meet me at the usual table.” Slide 2–3: simple use-case lines.

Caption sample

Your June catch-up spot, work reset, and quiet coffee break can all be the same table.

Assets needed
  • Table/store photo
  • Two drink setup
  • Hands/no-face option
  • Fallback: crop one strong table shot
Room notes: Moxie kept this because it feels specific to routine/local behavior. Riff recommends it as low-lift if assets exist.

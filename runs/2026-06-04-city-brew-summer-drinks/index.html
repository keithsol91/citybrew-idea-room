IDEA 01 · Instagram + Facebook carousel · Seasonal / Product feature / Saveable guide

City Brew Summer Field Guide

A saveable menu helper that turns the summer lineup into easy order decisions by matching each drink to a taste lane and real summer moment.

Visual Direction / Mockup

Warm local carousel. Cover: 3–4 cold drinks with headline 'City Brew Summer Field Guide.' Slides pair each approved drink with a use case: bright + icy, creamy + sweet, coffee-forward, usual-but-summer. Final slide: 'Save this for your next City Brew stop.'

On-Asset Copy

Save this for your next City Brew stop. / Order this if you want bright + icy. / Order this if you want creamy + sweet. / Your usual, but summer.

Caption Sample

Not every summer stop needs the same sip. Save the field guide and make your next City Brew order the easy decision.

Assets Needed

  • 3–4 approved summer drink photos
  • Approved drink names
  • Taste notes/flavor lanes
  • Branded carousel layout
  • Fallback: 3-panel static guide by flavor lane
Room notes: Recommended lead. Moxie kept it because it is useful and saveable; Riff flagged that it only works if each slide has drink, taste cue, best moment, and order reason.

IDEA 02 · Instagram Reels + Facebook Reels; Story cutdowns · Seasonal / Product feature / Order confidence

Barista Pick My Summer Drink

A quick barista-led recommendation Reel that reduces order anxiety and makes the summer lineup feel like a friendly City Brew recommendation.

Visual Direction / Mockup

9:16 handheld social Reel. First frame: barista holding three cups. Fast cuts to cup close-ups with preference lanes: not too sweet, dessert energy, my usual but summer. End with barista pointing to lineup and 'Which lane are you?'

On-Asset Copy

Pick your summer drink in 10 seconds. / Not too sweet → [drink]. / Dessert energy → [drink]. / My usual, but summer → [drink]. / Which lane are you?

Caption Sample

Not sure where to start? Tell us your drink lane and we’ll point you toward a summer order.

Assets Needed

  • One barista with on-camera permission
  • Three approved summer drinks
  • Taste lane mapping
  • Counter/window/store capture
  • Fallback: Story/static taste-lane graphic
Room notes: Strong trial-driver but not fully ready until product accuracy and barista availability are confirmed. Keep it human, not scripted menu-board energy.

IDEA 03 · Instagram Reel + Facebook Reel; carousel fallback · Seasonal / Lifestyle / Local routine

Mountain West Errand Loop

A routine-based post that makes the summer drink part of a believable Mountain West day instead of another generic cup beauty shot.

Visual Direction / Mockup

Quick errand-loop cuts: grocery bag, receipt, sunglasses, tote, counter pickup, parked cupholder still with condensation, keys. No filming while driving. Overlay copy frames City Brew as the required stop.

On-Asset Copy

The Mountain West errand loop has one required stop. / Required stop. / Errand fuel secured. / Back on track.

Caption Sample

Some errands need a little extra fuel. Make City Brew the stop that gets you through the loop.

Assets Needed

  • Featured summer drink
  • Errand props
  • Parked vehicle/cupholder shot or counter pickup
  • Condensation/ice close-up
  • Fallback: static cupholder image with overlay
Room notes: Moxie revised this away from generic cupholder beauty. Riff’s guardrail: object-led local routine, parked/static scenes only.

IDEA 04 · Instagram Stories sequence · Seasonal / Engagement / Support piece

Summer Drink Roll Call

A low-lift interactive Story unit that warms up the audience before the bigger Field Guide or Barista Reel and helps people choose a taste lane.

Visual Direction / Mockup

3–4 Story frames with poll/quiz stickers. Start with 'Roll call: what kind of summer order are you?' Then bright + icy vs creamy + sweet, coffee-forward vs afternoon treat, reveal/screenshot order lane.

On-Asset Copy

Roll call: what kind of summer order are you? / Bright + icy or creamy + sweet? / Screenshot your order lane. / Need help picking? Ask your barista at your next stop.

Caption Sample

Story copy only — keep it tap-friendly.

Assets Needed

  • 3–4 drink photos or approved flavor categories
  • IG Story poll/quiz stickers
  • Branded Story template
  • Fallback: single poll graphic with three taste lanes
Room notes: Kept as support, not hero. Best used to create engagement and lead into Barista Pick My Summer Drink or Field Guide.

IDEA 05 · Reel, carousel, or static lifestyle post · Seasonal / Lifestyle / Brand-building support

Before the Drive

A brand-building summer ritual post that positions City Brew as the stop before the day starts, using object-led lifestyle imagery rather than generic travel wallpaper.

Visual Direction / Mockup

Object-led flat lay: sunscreen, keys, sunglasses, tote/cooler, City Brew drink added to frame, ice/condensation close-up. Avoid active driving and avoid naming lakes/trails/towns unless approved.

On-Asset Copy

Before the lake bag. Before the cooler. Before the playlist. / One stop before the day starts.

Caption Sample

Whatever summer has on the list, start with the sip.

Assets Needed

  • One featured summer drink
  • Summer props
  • Approved lifestyle setting
  • Close-up ice/condensation/straw detail
  • Fallback: still-life flat lay carousel only
Room notes: Wildcard. Strong Mountain West role if done as specific object-led ritual; weak if it becomes generic lifestyle wallpaper.
